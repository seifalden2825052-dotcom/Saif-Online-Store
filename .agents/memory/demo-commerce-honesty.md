---
name: Demo commerce honesty
description: Trust boundary for the AUREN portfolio storefront checkout and fulfillment claims.
---

AUREN remains a portfolio demonstration until a real commerce backend and payment provider are integrated. Label checkout and confirmation as simulated, do not request card or CVC data, and do not imply that an order or shipment was created.

**Why:** A polished interface must not create false trust signals or collect sensitive-looking information when no payment or fulfillment system exists.

**How to apply:** Preserve this language and behavior in cart, checkout, confirmation, and policy copy. Replace it only as part of a verified real commerce integration.
